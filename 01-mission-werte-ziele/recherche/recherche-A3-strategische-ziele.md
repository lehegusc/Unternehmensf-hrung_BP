### Who Is Meg O'Neill?

O'Neill (b. 1970, American) is BP's **first outsider CEO** in the company's history. She spent **23 years at ExxonMobil** in technical, operational and leadership roles worldwide, then became **CEO of Australia's Woodside Energy**, before joining BP on 1 April 2026. [Yahoo Finance profile](http://www.finance.yahoo.com/quote/BP/profile/); [Rigzone](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/bp_must_make_better_use_of_shareholder_capital_ceo_says-05-oct-2026-184774-article/); [Mirror](https://www.mirror.co.uk/money/bp-profit-ceo-prices-bills-37826095)

Her appointment was framed as a mandate for rigorous capital discipline, outside perspective, and decisive simplification — breaking with BP's tradition of promoting internally. [BP Completes Castrol sale, Yahoo Finance, Nov 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/bp-completes-6-billion-castrol-083703268.html)

#### Priority 1: Bilanzstärke / Strengthening the Balance Sheet

> *"This is good progress, but we are still too leveraged and further strengthening of the balance sheet remains our **number one job**."*
> — Meg O'Neill, Q3 2026 [[bp.com]](https://www.bp.com/en-GB/investors/results-reporting-and-presentations/quarterly-results-and-webcast)

**What it means:** Reducing net debt, hybrid bond obligations, lease liabilities, and the legacy Deepwater Horizon ("Gulf of America") settlement liabilities. O'Neill has explicitly said debt reduction takes priority over share buybacks and capex increases alike.

**Concrete KPIs and progress (as of Q3 2026):**
- Net debt fell from **$22.3bn** (end-Q2 2026) to **$13.87bn** (end-Q3 2026) — a single-quarter drop of **$8.4bn**, driven by operating cash flow of $14.8bn, the Castrol sale proceeds, and a hybrid bond redemption. [Reuters, Oct 30 2026](https://www.reuters.com/business/bp-posts-net-income-846-bln-beating-expectations-2026-10-30/)
- This puts BP **below its own 2027 target range** of $14–18bn — one year ahead of schedule. [Morningstar/Alliance News](https://www.morningstar.com/news/alliance-news/1793351606965462700/bp-hits-debt-target-a-year-ahead-of-schedule-as-profit-beats-forecast)
- BP targets **total financial obligations** (net debt + hybrids + leases + Gulf of America liabilities) of **$33bn–$35bn by end-2026**. [Investing.com](https://au.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/earnings-call-transcript-bp-beats-q3-2026-estimates-but-shares-slip-after-hours-93CH-4731658)
- O'Neill has confirmed **share buybacks remain suspended** until the balance sheet is sufficiently strengthened, citing that BP still "carries more debt than it can afford in a low price scenario." [Reuters, Oct 30 2026](https://www.reuters.com/business/bp-posts-net-income-846-bln-beating-expectations-2026-10-30/)

#### Priority 2: Portfolio vereinfachen / Simplifying the Portfolio

> *"We are moving at speed to simplify bp's portfolio to assets that best fit our integrated model and deliver the most competitive cash and returns."*
> — BP 3Q26 stock exchange announcement [[MarketScreener]](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/bp-3q-2026-stock-exchange-announcement-ce785fdbd88bf423)

**What it means:** Selling or exiting assets that do not fit the integrated oil-and-gas model, generate sub-competitive returns, or dilute margins. The stated criterion is capital efficiency and return quality, not heritage or history.

**Concrete transactions completed or announced by Q3 2026:**
- **Castrol lubricants:** 65% stake sold to US private equity firm Stonepeak for **~$6 billion**, completed November 2026. BP retains 35%. [Reuters, Nov 2 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/bp-completes-sale-65-castrol-stake-stonepeak-2026-11-02/); [Yahoo Finance](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/bp-completes-6-billion-castrol-083703268.html)
- **Gelsenkirchen refinery** (Germany): sale completed July 2026. [Investing.com slides](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bp-q3-2026-slides-net-debt-falls-below-target-portfolio-reshapes-93CH-4947293)
- **Austria mobility & convenience business**: sale agreed July 2026. [Investing.com]
- **Bay du Nord** (Canada offshore): sold its non-operated interest. [Investing.com]; [el-fondo.com](https://www.el-fondo.com/en/news/bp-exits-bay-du-nord-offshore-oil-project-in-canada)
- **UK North Sea**: BP launched a **full sale process for all its UK North Sea oil and gas assets**, attracting significant interest. [Morningstar/Dow Jones](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202610131204/bps-new-ceo-to-outline-strategy-as-balance-sheet-strengthens-2nd-update)
- **Archaea Energy** (US biogas): sale process underway. [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-what-bp-bp-said-on-its-q3-earnings-call-2026-10-30/)
- **Lightsource bp** (solar): sale process underway. [MarketBeat]
- **Kirkuk field** (Iraq): BP agreed to bring in partners (de-risk). [Investing.com slides]
- **$20 billion total divestment programme** by 2027 (inherited from Feb 2025 reset). [el-fondo.com](https://www.el-fondo.com/en/news/bp-exits-bay-du-nord-offshore-oil-project-in-canada)

#### Priority 3: Investitionsdisziplin / Investing with Discipline

> *"Now is not the time to increase spending. We need to execute."*
> — Meg O'Neill, Energy Intelligence Forum, London, October 5, 2026 [[EnergyVoice]](https://www.energyvoice.com/oilandgas/604738/bp-debt-meg-oneill-hydrogen/)

**What it means:** Capital projects compete purely on expected returns and cash generation. Capex is held flat or reduced while disposals are prioritised.

**Concrete KPIs:**
- Full-year **2026 capex guidance: $13.5bn–$14.0bn** (reduced from prior plans). [BP 3Q26 results](https://www.bp.com/en-US/press-and-publications/press-releases/third-quarter-2026-results); [stocktitan.net](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/BP/6-k-bp-plc-current-report-foreign-issuer-c34393cca987.html)
- Q3 2026 actual capex: **$3.036bn** (in line with guidance). [bp.com Q3 results]
- Of total capex, **~$10bn/year** goes to upstream oil & gas (inherited from Feb 2025 reset). [Post-Journal/AP wire](https://www.post-journal.com/wire/?category=5216&ID=357336)
- **Low-carbon/transition spending held to $1.5–$2.0bn/year**, of which low-carbon energy specifically is **under $0.8bn**. [projectfifty4.com](https://projectfifty4.com/oil-majors-low-carbon-capex-comparison-2026/)
- **Adjusted free cash flow growth >20%/year** from 2024 to 2027 (one of the four primary 2027 targets). [tickerscout.ai](https://tickerscout.ai/bp/narrative/)
- **ROACE (Return on Average Capital Employed) above 16%** by 2027 (a remaining target). [tickerscout.ai]

#### Priority 4: Operative Performance / Improving Operational Performance

**What it means:** Reducing unplanned outages, improving plant reliability, lifting production efficiency — translating better macro conditions into actual earnings rather than losing them to operational failures.

**Concrete KPIs (Q3 2026 progress):**
- **Upstream plant reliability:** improved to **95.3%** in Q3 2026, from **92.4%** in Q2 2026. [bp.com Q3 results](https://www.bp.com/en-US/press-and-publications/press-releases/third-quarter-2026-results)
- **Refining availability:** improved to **96.5%** in Q3 2026, from **94.7%** in Q2. [bp.com]
- **Upstream production:** ~2.2 million boe/d (broadly flat vs. Q2). Full-year 2026 guidance: **2,210–2,250 mboe/d**. [bp.com]
- O'Neill explicitly noted in Q3 commentary: *"improved operational performance allowed us to effectively capture the higher commodity price environment."* [The Globe and Mail transcript](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/markets-news/motley/8559062/bp-bp-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

#### Priority 5: Accountability (Hardwiring a Performance Culture)

**What it means:** Creating clearer personal accountability for results across BP's management layers, reducing bureaucracy, and embedding a "performance culture" that had allegedly been diluted in the transition era.

**Specific framing (from Q3 2026 earnings):**

> *"Hardwire a stronger performance culture across BP, creating clearer accountability for results."*
> — Meg O'Neill, Q3 2026 Earnings Call [[Globe and Mail transcript]](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/markets-news/motley/8559062/bp-bp-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

**Actions taken:**
- New senior management structure; brought in new board chair.
- Reorganisation into two segments with clearer ownership.
- Asset sales framed as removing low-accountability "drag" from the portfolio.
- The **Energy Intelligence** noted that O'Neill had "revamped the company's organisational structure, shed assets and people, and been travelling the world as the public face of a company that had long lacked a clear, strategic direction." [EnergyIntel, Oct 8 2026](https://www.energyintel.com/000001a1-16ef-ded0-ade1-9fefa0e10000)

### 3.5 Cost Reduction / Efficiency Targets

This is the most technically layered financial target set.

**The four primary 2027 targets (set February 2025):**

| Target | Description | Status (Q3 2026) |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Adjusted FCF growth **>20%/year** from 2024–2027 (price-adjusted) | In progress |
| 2 | Net debt **$14–$18bn** by end-2027 | ✅ **Delivered early** ($13.87bn) |
| 3 | Structural cost reductions **$5.5–$6.5bn** vs. 2023 by end-2027 | ✅ **Target range delivered** |
| 4 | ROACE **>16%** by end-2027 | In progress |

Sources: [tickerscout.ai narrative](https://tickerscout.ai/bp/narrative/); [BP Q3 2026 results](https://www.bp.com/en-US/press-and-publications/press-releases/third-quarter-2026-results)

**Nuance on cost reductions:** BP's CFO Kate Thomson explained at Q3 2026 that $4bn of structural savings have been delivered since 2023, but these have been **largely offset by headwinds**:
- ~**$1.5bn** of inflation (2.5%/year)
- ~**$800m** from acquired cost bases
- ~**$700m** from activity choices (e.g. bpx production)

The broader target including the Castrol and Gelsenkirchen exits is **$6.5–$7.5bn** of structural cost reduction by 2027. [Investing.com transcript](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/earnings-call-transcript-bp-beats-q3-2026-estimates-but-shares-slip-after-hours-93CH-4947273); [X/Finsee analysis](https://x.com/Finsee_main/status/2116140711619768571)

**Absolute cost base target:** BP aims for an absolute cost base of approximately **$18bn in 2027**. [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3020625/bp-q3-earnings-call-highlights-balance-sheet-and-strategic-reset)

## PART 4 — CLIMATE AND ENERGY TRANSITION GOALS

### 4.1 What Still Stands

- **Net-zero by 2050 ambition**: BP has **retained** its stated ambition of net zero by 2050 for its own operations and for the carbon intensity of the energy it sells. [GreenAlchemist](https://www.greenalchemist.co.uk/eco-company-reviews/9676-bp-sustainability); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/BP-PLC)
- **Scope 1 & 2 emissions targets**: BP still reports and tracks these. North Sea Scope 1 emissions were 26% lower than the 2019 baseline by 2025. [bp.com North Sea page](https://www.bp.com/en-GB/about-us/what-we-do/oil-and-gas/north-sea-oil-and-gas)
- **Selective low-carbon projects**: CCS, hydrogen (5–10 projects targeted for the decade, per Auchincloss), and a residual offshore wind portfolio via the JERA Nex bp joint venture. [FuelCellChina](https://www.fuelcellchina.com/Industry_information_details/4650.html); [bp.com offshore wind](https://www.bp.com/en-GB/about-us/what-we-do/lower-carbon-energy/offshore-wind)

### 4.2 What Was Rolled Back — The February 2025 "Fundamental Reset"

Auchincloss announced on **26 February 2025** (explicitly described as a "fundamental reset"):

> *"Today we have **fundamentally reset bp's strategy**. We are reducing and reallocating capital expenditure to our highest-returning businesses to drive growth."*
> — Murray Auchincloss, February 26, 2025 [[projectfifty4.com]](https://projectfifty4.com/oil-majors-low-carbon-capex-comparison-2026/)

> *"We went too far, too fast towards renewables."*
> — Murray Auchincloss, February 2025 [[The Ecologist via projectfifty4]](https://theecologist.org/2026/oct/19/extractivists-foreign-office)

**Key rollbacks:**

| What Was Dropped/Reduced | Old Ambition | New Position |
|--------------------------|-------------|--------------|
| **50 GW renewables target by 2030** | 50 GW | **Scrapped** |
| **Transition/low-carbon annual capex** | >$5bn/year | Cut to **$1.5–$2bn/year** |
| **Oil and gas production decline target** (40% cut by 2030) | -40% by 2030 | **Reversed** — output to remain broadly flat or grow |
| **Scope 3 production emissions target** | Explicit production-linked target | **Dropped** |
| **US onshore wind assets** | Owned | **Sold** |

Sources: [Stratrix analysis](https://www.stratrix.com/reversals/bp-s-renewables-retreat-and-deepwater); [GreenAlchemist](https://www.greenalchemist.co.uk/eco-company-reviews/9676-bp-sustainability); [National Review/Stratrix](https://www.nationalreview.com/corner/another-green-waste/); [The Strategy Story](https://thestrategystory.com/blog/bp-business-model-in-2026-how-does-bp-make-money/)

### 4.3 Oil Production Targets

- Under the 2025 reset, BP **raised its oil and gas production target to 2.3–2.5 million boe/d by 2030** (vs. the prior goal of ~1.5 million boe/d). [Stratrix](https://www.stratrix.com/reversals/bp-s-renewables-retreat-and-deepwater); [KoalaGains](https://koalagains.com/stocks/XETRA/BPE5/future-performance)
- **Key US growth driver**: BPX Energy (US onshore) has a target to grow to **~1 million boe/d by 2030**. [TwelfthMagpie](https://www.twelfthmagpie.com/2026/10/19/the-bp-share-price-has-soared-50-in-a-year-could-it-still-hit-700p/)
- **Iraq Kirkuk redevelopment** is another major growth pillar.
- **Actual 2025 production**: 2,312 mboe/d. [BP Q3 2026 results]
- **2026 full-year guidance**: **2,210–2,250 mboe/d** — *lower* than 2025 due to divestments (North Sea Culzean sale, Latin America equity reduction, Middle East disruptions). [BP Q3 2026 results press release](https://www.bp.com/en-US/press-and-publications/press-releases/third-quarter-2026-results); [RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/3706152/bp-q3-rc-profit-climbs-updates-fy26-production-view.aspx)

> **⚠️ Contradiction/nuance**: BP's *long-term* target is to grow production to 2.3–2.5 million boe/d by 2030, but *near-term* production is declining in 2026 due to divestments. These are consistent — the production target refers to organic growth assets, while divestments reduce the reported headline number.

### 4.4 Renewable Energy — What Was Cut and What Remains

**What was cut:**
- **Lightsource bp** (solar): being sold (sale process launched July 2026). [MarketBeat]
- **Offshore wind** (sole ownership): placed into the **JERA Nex bp joint venture** — a "capital-light" model where BP no longer bears full development cost. Projects include Mona, Morven, Flora, and Gunfleet Sands in the UK. [bp.com offshore wind page](https://www.bp.com/en-GB/about-us/what-we-do/lower-carbon-energy/offshore-wind); [Disruption Banking](https://www.disruptionbanking.com/2026/10/08/big-oils-50bn-quarter-did-not-restart-its-esg-transition-budget/)
- **US onshore wind**: already sold. [Disruption Banking]

**What remains:**
- CCS, hydrogen, JERA Nex bp offshore wind JV, Archaea biogas (being sold), biofuels and EV charging (folded into downstream in new segment model).
- Per the reorganisation announcement, *"renewable businesses, including solar and offshore wind, will sit within the Technology function as BP continues to advance a capital-light model in these areas."* [Rigzone, Oct 13 2026](https://www.rigzone.com/news/bp_slashes_debt-13-oct-2026-184833-article/)

[StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/BP/6-k-bp-plc-current-report-foreign-issuer-c34393cca987.html); [CityAM](https://www.cityam.com/bp-shares-lift-as-oil-giant-cheers-surge-in-profit/)

## PART 5 — STRUCTURAL REORGANISATION

### 5.1 From Three Segments to Two

**Announced**: 9 June 2026
**Effective for external financial reporting**: 1 January 2027
**Old structure** (still used for external reporting through 31 December 2026):
1. Oil Production & Operations
2. Gas & Low Carbon Energy
3. Customers & Products
(+ Rosneft as separate segment)

**New structure** (from 1 January 2027):
1. **Upstream** — BP's oil and gas regions (exploration, development, production), plus joint ventures, RNG (biogas), and CCS
2. **Downstream** — Refining, terminals, pipelines, mobility & convenience, biofuels, aviation, hydrogen, and Castrol (residual stake)
3. **Supply, Trading and Shipping** — Continues to operate across both segments

Sources: [Yahoo Finance/BP Slashes Debt](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/bp-slashes-debt-175618389.html); [BP Q3 results](https://www.bp.com/en-US/press-and-publications/press-releases/third-quarter-2026-results)

> *"This organisational change builds on the concrete actions BP is taking to simplify its portfolio, reduce costs, maintain tight capex discipline and strengthen its balance sheet — all in service of growing value and returns for shareholders."*

### 5.2 What Happened to "Low Carbon Energy"?

The "Gas & Low Carbon Energy" segment is being **dissolved** as a standalone reporting unit. Its constituent parts are redistributed:
- **Gas upstream** (LNG, gas regions) → New **Upstream** segment
- **Archaea biogas (RNG)** → New **Upstream** segment (then being sold)
- **Solar (Lightsource bp)** → **Technology function** (capital-light model, then being sold)
- **Offshore wind (JERA Nex bp JV)** → **Technology function** (capital-light)
- **Hydrogen, CCS** → New **Downstream** segment
- **Gas trading** → Supply, Trading and Shipping

## PART 6 — STRATEGIC COMPARISON: LOONEY vs. AUCHINCLOSS vs. O'NEILL

### 6.1 Bernard Looney (Feb 2020 – Sep 2023): The Green Pivot

Looney's February 2020 strategy was the most radical departure in BP's history.

| Dimension | Looney Ambition |
|-----------|----------------|
| Corporate identity | "Integrated energy company" (not an oil company) |
| Oil & gas production | **Cut 40% by 2030** (~1 million boe/d reduction) |
| Net zero | By **2050** for operations + carbon intensity of energy sold |
| Renewables | Build to **~50 GW by 2030** |
| Low-carbon capex | Increase to ~**$5bn/year** |
| Scope 3 emissions | Reduce intensity of energy products sold |
| Strategy summary | De-emphasise hydrocarbons; lead the energy transition |

Sources: [Stratrix](https://www.stratrix.com/reversals/bp-s-renewables-retreat-and-deepwater); [Affarsvarlden](https://www.affarsvarlden.se/kronika/bp-s-grona-fiasko-nedskrivning-pa-upp-till-45-miljarder); [LNG Industry editorial 2020](https://www.lngindustry.com/magazine/tanks-terminals/march-2020/editorial-comment/); [IMD case study](https://www.imd.org/research-knowledge/board/case-studies/bp-s-transformation-strategy-under-pressure-a-pivotal-moment-for-the-board-s-chair/)

**Note on Scope 3 under Looney**: The original Looney strategy included targets on the carbon intensity of energy BP sold to customers, but the production-decline-linked Scope 3 ambition was the closest to a customer-emissions target. This was already being softened in 2022–2023 under shareholder and operational pressure before Looney departed.

### 6.2 Murray Auchincloss (Jan 2024 – Dec 2025): The Partial Retreat

Auchincloss began walking back the Looney transition strategy from the day he took over, culminating in the **February 26, 2025 "fundamental reset"**:

| Dimension | Auchincloss Reset (Feb 2025) |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Corporate identity | Integrated oil and gas company |
| Oil & gas investment | **+20%** to ~$10bn/year |
| Renewables/transition capex | Cut to **$1.5–2bn/year** |
| Oil production target | **Reversed**: 2.3–2.5 million boe/d by 2030 (growth, not decline) |
| 50 GW renewables target | **Scrapped** |
| Scope 3 production target | **Dropped** |
| Net zero 2050 | Retained (as high-level ambition) |
| Financial frame | Four 2027 targets (net debt, FCF growth, cost reductions, ROACE) |
| Divestments | $20bn programme by 2027 |

Sources: [Post-Journal/AP](https://www.post-journal.com/wire/?category=5216&ID=357336); [tickerscout.ai](https://tickerscout.ai/bp/narrative/); [Stratrix](https://www.stratrix.com/reversals/bp-s-renewables-retreat-and-deepwater)

**Context**: Auchincloss was under sustained pressure from **Elliott Management** (activist hedge fund) and broader shareholder dissatisfaction with BP's share price underperformance vs. peers. His departure in December 2025 was described as unexpected after "less than two years in the permanent role." [The Daily Energy](https://thedailyenergy.com/bp/)

### 6.3 Meg O'Neill (April 2026 – present): Inherited Reset, Deeper Simplification

O'Neill **inherited** the Auchincloss reset and has accelerated portfolio simplification and balance sheet repair, while deferring her own comprehensive strategic framework to December 2026.

| Dimension | O'Neill Approach (as of Q3 2026) |
|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Corporate identity | "Simpler, stronger, more valuable bp" — integrated oil & gas |
| Oil & gas | Continues ~$10bn/year upstream investment; production broadly flat near-term due to divestments |
| Renewables/transition | Further impairments; Lightsource bp and Archaea being sold; residual offshore wind in capital-light JV |
| Balance sheet | **Top priority** — net debt already below 2027 target |
| Buybacks | Suspended — not being restarted until balance sheet improves |
| Portfolio | Most aggressive simplification phase — Castrol, Gelsenkirchen, UK North Sea, Austria M&C all divested/in progress |
| Segment structure | Reorganised from 3 to 2 segments (effective 2027) |
| Breakup option | **Categorically denied** by O'Neill on Oct 30, 2026 |
| Longer-term strategy | To be unveiled **December 15, 2026** in New York |

Sources: [Reuters Oct 30 2026](https://www.reuters.com/business/bp-posts-net-income-846-bln-beating-expectations-2026-10-30/); [Investing.com/BP CEO denies breakup](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bp-ceo-denies-breakup-discussions-4988151); [bp.com Q3 results](https://www.bp.com/en-US/press-and-publications/press-releases/third-quarter-2026-results)

## PART 7 — ADDITIONAL CONTEXT AND FLAGS

### Breakup Speculation (October 2026)

On October 28, 2026, Semafor reported that **some BP board members were considering a breakup** of the company into separate upstream and downstream entities, amid continued shareholder pressure. O'Neill **categorically denied** any such discussions on the Q3 earnings call on October 30, 2026:

> *"Board discussions reaffirm almost the imperative of having an integrated oil and gas company generating value from Upstream, Downstream, and Trading."*
> — Meg O'Neill, Q3 2026 Q&A [[Seeking Alpha news](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4650396-bp-must-make-better-use-of-investor-capital-ceo-oneill-says); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bp-ceo-denies-breakup-discussions-4988151)]

### Elliott Management Pressure

US activist hedge fund **Elliott Management** has been a significant force pushing BP toward capital discipline, cost cuts, and balance sheet repair. Its presence helped drive both the February 2025 reset and the board changes of 2026. [Yorkshire Post](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/business/bps-new-ceo-to-reveal-fresh-strategy-after-it-receives-boost-from-soaring-crude-and-gas-prices-9283654)

### Geopolitical Tailwind (Late 2026)

BP's Q3 2026 record profit was heavily aided by external factors: higher oil and gas prices driven by Middle East conflict (including the Strait of Hormuz closures and Iran war), and surging refining margins. O'Neill acknowledged this:

> *"These are dynamic times with volatility and disruption to trade flows continuing to define energy markets."*
> [[France24]](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261030-bp-net-profit-quadruples-as-mideast-war-lifts-oil-prices)

### ⚠️ Uncertainty Flags for Academic Work

1. **December 15, 2026 strategy day is not yet happened** (as of research date, early November 2026). All current O'Neill "priorities" are interim/transitional — her full strategic framework with new multi-year targets will be presented then.
2. The **exact wording** of "five priorities" differs slightly across sources. The five consistently cited are: (1) strengthen balance sheet, (2) maximise cash from core business, (3) simplify portfolio, (4) improve operational performance, (5) hardwire performance culture / accountability. Some sources list them in slightly different order.
3. **Scope 3 target**: The February 2025 reset "dropped" BP's production-linked Scope 3 target, but BP still reports Scope 3 emissions and retains a net-zero 2050 commitment that arguably implies Scope 3 ambition over the long run. This creates tension in corporate reporting.
4. **Production numbers**: 2026 production is *lower* than 2025 due to divestments, which may appear to contradict "growth" strategy. The reconciliation is that core business growth offsets portfolio reshaping.
5. The **Auchincloss departure** is described by sources variously as a "resignation", a "stepping down", and an "unexpected exit." The precise circumstances have not been publicly disclosed in full.
