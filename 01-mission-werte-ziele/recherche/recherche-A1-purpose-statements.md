## OVERVIEW: THE FULL LINEAGE OF BP PURPOSE STATEMENTS

| Era | CEO | Years | Purpose/Mission Statement |
|-----|-----|-------|---------------------------|

| "Beyond Petroleum" | John Browne | 2000–2007 | **"Beyond Petroleum"** (marketing slogan/rebranding, not a formal "purpose") |
| Post-Deepwater | Tony Hayward / Bob Dudley | 2007–2018 | No single formal purpose statement; mission language around "safe, reliable energy" |

## QUESTION 1: THE OLD PURPOSE STATEMENT — "REIMAGINING ENERGY FOR PEOPLE AND OUR PLANET"

### 1.1 Exact Wording

BP's official purpose statement, as stated in every BP document from 2020–2024, was:

> **"Our purpose is reimagining energy for people and our planet."**

This is confirmed in the BP press release of 12 February 2020 [https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/bernard-looney-announces-new-ambition-for-bp], in the BP 2020 Annual Report [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/country-sites/nl-nl/netherlands/home/documents/perscentrum/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2020.pdf], and in BP's own submission to New Zealand's Climate Change Commission [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/country-sites/en_nz/new-zealand/home/documents/bpsubmission-climate-change-commission-draftadvice.pdf], which states: *"On 12 February 2020 bp adopted a new purpose – 'reimagining energy for people and our planet'."*

### 1.2 When Was It Adopted? — The Precise Date and a Nuance

**Official adoption date: 12 February 2020.** This was the day Bernard Looney gave his inaugural strategy speech, titled *"Reimagining Energy, Reinventing BP,"* at BP's London headquarters, just one week after taking over as CEO on 5 February 2020. [https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/bernard-looney-announces-new-ambition-for-bp]

**Important nuance:** The purpose wording already appeared in the BP 2019 Annual Report (for financial year 2019, published in early 2020). BP's AR2019 opens with: *"We enter a new decade with a new company purpose: to reimagine energy for people and our planet."* [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/country-sites/nl-nl/netherlands/home/documents/perscentrum/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2019.pdf] [https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/bp--bp./bp-annual-report-and-form-20-f-2019/6032313]

BP's own Section 172 governance statement explains this apparent overlap: *"Following the announcement of Bernard Looney's appointment as chief executive officer (CEO) in October 2019, the board engaged with Bernard and the leadership team to develop the company's new purpose, net zero ambition and aims."* [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/investors/ar-2019-section-172-statement.pdf] In other words, the purpose was developed by the board and the incoming CEO from October 2019 onwards, but it was formally announced by Looney publicly on 12 February 2020.

The AR2019 was published after 12 February 2020 (it covers the year ending 31 December 2019 but was published in early 2020 under Looney's name). The BP Sustainability Report 2019 [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/country-sites/de_at/austria/home/news/publikationen/bp-sustainability-report-2019.pdf] also carries the new purpose.

### 1.3 Under Which CEO?

**Bernard Looney** — BP's Chief Executive Officer, effective 5 February 2020. He was an Irish engineer who had risen through BP's upstream division. His predecessor Bob Dudley retired on 31 March 2020 following the announcement in October 2019.

### 1.4 Was It Called "Purpose," "Mission," or Something Else?

BP consistently and explicitly called it a **"purpose"** — not a "mission" or "vision." The press release headline reads: *"BP sets ambition for net zero by 2050, fundamentally changing organisation to deliver"* and the body states: **"BP's new purpose is reimagining energy for people and our planet."** [https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/bernard-looney-announces-new-ambition-for-bp] The word "purpose" was deliberately chosen, reflecting the broader trend in corporate governance toward purpose-driven business.

The accompanying formal document was also titled **"Reimagining energy, reinventing BP"** — the speech Looney delivered on 12 February 2020. [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/news-and-insights/speeches/reimagining-energy-reinventing-bp.pdf]

### 1.5 Strategic Context at Adoption

Looney's speech on 12 February 2020 — just one week after taking office — was a landmark event, delivered to a room of investors, journalists, and staff at BP's London headquarters. Key contextual elements:

- **Net-zero ambition:** The purpose was paired with BP's commitment to become *"a net zero company by 2050 or sooner, and to help the world get to net zero."* This made BP the first oil major to adopt such an ambitious and comprehensive net-zero ambition at the time.

- **Production cuts:** Looney announced BP would cut oil and gas production by **40% by 2030** compared to 2019 levels.

- **Organizational restructure:** BP announced a *"fundamental reorganisation,"* moving from a siloed upstream/downstream model to an integrated one with four business groups, eliminating two layers of management.

- **Energy transition framing:** Looney declared: *"Providing the world with clean, reliable, affordable energy will require nothing less than reimagining energy. Today, that becomes BP's new purpose."* [BBC, 12 Feb 2020: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/business-51475379]

- **Climate imperative:** Looney framed the change in terms of societal expectations: *"The world's carbon budget is finite and running out fast; we need a rapid transition to net zero. We all want energy that is reliable and affordable, but that is no longer enough. It must also be cleaner."* [AP, 12 Feb 2020: https://apnews.com/1f66df4cb23e89da039517163ef80dc4]

- **Strategy tagline:** The overarching strategy was later branded *"performing while transforming"* — delivering near-term cash while investing in the energy transition. [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/business-54702562]

- **McKinsey observation:** McKinsey noted in November 2021 that *"at the start of 2020, new CEO Bernard Looney announced an updated purpose for the company: to reimagine energy for people and the planet and to be a net-zero company by 2050 or sooner."* [https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/oil-and-gas/our-insights/bp-an-agility-pioneer-in-the-energy-industry]

## QUESTION 2: THE NEW PURPOSE STATEMENT — "DELIVERING ENERGY TO THE WORLD, TODAY AND TOMORROW"

### 2.1 Exact Wording

BP's current official purpose statement, as of 26 February 2025, is:

> **"Our purpose is to deliver energy to the world, today and tomorrow."**

Or equivalently (the statement appears in various slightly reformulated but synonymous forms):

> **"Delivering energy to the world, today and tomorrow."**

This is confirmed in the "People and Planet Aims at a Glance" document (updated 26 February 2025) [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/1mONt6arxEWFez3nYtupga/c68e1763ba224c58817b226d86c8e086/bp-people-and-planet-aims-at-a-glance.pdf], the "Net Zero Aims at a Glance" document [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/7M1AN8ub2AIK3XyqAOoY0T/8dd2e302efddb46921b641d5e85c636c/bp-net-zero-aims-at-a-glance.pdf], the BP Annual Report 2024 [https://bp-com-test3.navitas.bpglobal.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/investors/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2024.pdf], and BP's current "Who We Are" webpage [https://www.bp.com/about-us/who-we-are].

### 2.2 Exact Date and Context

The new purpose was formally established on **26 February 2025**, as part of BP's **Capital Markets Update (investor day)**. BP's own "People and Planet Aims" document carries the explicit date stamp: *"As updated 26 February 2025."* The document also notes: *"This document reflects changes since 14 March 2024 and reflects what we announced as part of our strategy reset at a Capital Markets Update on 26 February 2025."* [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/1mONt6arxEWFez3nYtupga/c68e1763ba224c58817b226d86c8e086/bp-people-and-planet-aims-at-a-glance.pdf]

The investor event itself was a full Capital Markets Update presentation at BP's London headquarters, presented by Murray Auchincloss (CEO), Kate Thomson (CFO, appointed alongside the reset), and members of the leadership team. [https://www.bp.com/en-US/investors/results-reporting-and-presentations/archive-of-results-reports-and-presentations/2025]

The press release issued that day was titled: **"Growing shareholder value: a reset bp"** and published at 10:00 on 26 February 2025. [https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/growing-shareholder-value-a-reset-bp; also LSE filing: https://www.londonstockexchange.com/en/news/article/BP./growing-shareholder-value-a-reset-bp/16916021]

**Important note on the annual report publication date:** The BP Annual Report 2024 (for financial year 2024) was also published on **11 February 2025** [https://www.bp.com/en-US/investors/results-reporting-and-presentations/archive-of-results-reports-and-presentations/2024], and already carries the new purpose wording: *"Our purpose is to deliver energy to the world, today and tomorrow."* [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/4YdMeDFNDUgV33gMyfrUK7/e6a7feaf8b863b24e985f8b4550699e9/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2024.pdf] This is 15 days before the February 26 Capital Markets Update, which creates a minor ambiguity: the Annual Report was finalised and published ahead of the investor day but the formal strategic documents accompanying the reset carry the February 26 date. This suggests the annual report was finalized with the knowledge of the upcoming strategy reset, or that the purpose change was decided before the investor day was held. Chair Helge Lund's letter in the AR2024 references *"supplying the energy the world needs today and helping build out the energy system of tomorrow"* [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/investors/bp-ar2024-chairman-letter.pdf], using language fully consistent with the new purpose.

### 2.3 Who Announced It?

**Murray Auchincloss** — BP's CEO, who had been permanent CEO since **17 January 2024** (having served as interim CEO from September 2023). Auchincloss personally delivered the Capital Markets Update presentation on 26 February 2025. He was accompanied by CFO Kate Thomson.

Chair **Helge Lund** provided board endorsement in the press release statement: *"The board believes that this is an important strategic reset for bp and is confident that it, together with rigorous performance management, will deliver improved performance and sustainable value for bp's shareholders. Over the past 12 months, we have worked closely with Murray and his team as they have developed the new direction, ensuring it reflects the significant changes we have seen in energy markets and our purpose of delivering energy to the world, today and tomorrow."* [https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/growing-shareholder-value-a-reset-bp]

### 2.4 How Was the Change Communicated?

The purpose change was embedded within the **capital markets update / investor day** event on 26 February 2025. It was NOT announced as a standalone "purpose change" press release — rather, the new purpose appeared as the framing for BP's broader strategic reset. The vehicle was:

1. **Press release titled "Growing shareholder value: a reset bp"** — published 26 February 2025
2. **Capital markets update presentation** — delivered by Auchincloss, Thomson, and the leadership team
3. **Updated BP documents** — "People and Planet Aims at a Glance" and "Net Zero Aims at a Glance" both updated 26 February 2025
4. **BP Annual Report 2024** — already published 11 February 2025 with new purpose wording

Media covered the investor day extensively as a fossil fuel pivot story (Reuters, FT, BBC, CNBC, Bloomberg all published same-day). However, the specific purpose wording change received less dedicated coverage than the broader strategic reset.

## QUESTION 3: THE TRANSITION/CHANGE PROCESS

### 3.1 The Intermediate Period (2023–2025)

There was a meaningful **intermediate period** of strategic recalibration between the pure Looney-era strategy and the Auchincloss 2025 reset:

**February 2023 (under Looney):** BP scaled back its oil and gas production reduction target from **40% to 25% by 2030**, while simultaneously announcing plans to invest more in oil and gas and increase capital expenditure to oil and gas businesses. [Washington Post, 7 Feb 2023: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2023/02/07/bp-climate-emissions-oil-profits/] The purpose statement remained unchanged at this point.

**September 2023 — Looney Resignation:** Bernard Looney resigned on 12 September 2023, effective immediately, after admitting he *"was not fully transparent"* in disclosures about past personal relationships with colleagues. [Reuters, 13 Sep 2023: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/bp-ceo-bernard-looney-resign-ft-2023-09-12/; CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2023/09/12/bp-ceo-bernard-looney-will-resign-report-says.html] This was a governance issue, not a strategic one, but it opened the door to more substantive strategic change.

Murray Auchincloss (BP's CFO) was immediately appointed **interim CEO**, and at a town hall the following day explicitly pledged strategic continuity: *"Our strategy — from international oil company to integrated energy company — does not change."* [Reuters, 13 Sep 2023: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/bps-interim-ceo-stick-energy-transition-after-looneys-exit-sources-2023-09-13/]

**January 2024 — Auchincloss Made Permanent CEO:** On 17 January 2024, BP announced Auchincloss as permanent CEO. He again pledged strategy continuity on appointment. [Reuters, 17 Jan 2024: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/bp-names-auchincloss-permanent-ceo-2024-01-17/]

**October 2024 — Strategy Shift Signals Emerge:** Reuters reported in October 2024 that BP had *"abandoned a target to cut oil and gas output by 2030"* as Auchincloss scaled back the energy transition strategy. [Reuters, 7 Oct 2024: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/bp-drops-oil-output-target-strategy-reset-sources-say-2024-10-07/]

**February 2025 — Full Reset:** The purpose was formally changed on 26 February 2025 alongside the comprehensive strategy reset.

### 3.2 BP's Stated Reasoning for the Change

Auchincloss delivered the key framing in his Capital Markets Update presentation on 26 February 2025, and subsequently in media interviews. The core reasoning, in his own words:

> **"Our optimism for a fast transition was misplaced, and we went too far, too fast."**
> — Murray Auchincloss, Capital Markets Update, 26 February 2025 [ESG Dive: https://www.esgdive.com/news/bp-cuts-renewable-spending-doubles-down-on-fossil-fuels-in-strategy-reset/741287/; Recharge News: https://www.rechargenews.com/markets/misplaced-optimism-bp-got-it-wrong-over-energy-transition-says-ceo/2-1-1785180]

> **"The pace of transition and decarbonisation, while important, was not as fast as envisioned."**
> — Murray Auchincloss, 26 February 2025 [Wind Power Monthly: https://www.windpowermonthly.com/article/1907947/bp-slashes-renewables-plans-boosting-fossil-fuels]

BBC reported: *"Murray Auchincloss, BP's chief executive, said the energy giant had gone 'too far, too fast' in the transition away from fossil fuels, and that its faith in green energy was 'misplaced'."* [BBC, 26 Feb 2025: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3374ekd11po]

**Macro factors cited by Auchincloss:**
- Covid-19 pandemic and Russia's invasion of Ukraine created energy price pressures
- These pressures caused governments to prioritise lower-cost energy, slowing transition investment
- EV adoption was slower than anticipated
- Returns from wind and solar were weaker than expected (BP generated 15-20% returns on fossil fuel investments vs. 6-8% from renewables) [SMH: https://www.smh.com.au/business/companies/big-oil-s-green-dream-has-turned-into-a-multibillion-dollar-nightmare-20241218-p5kz7e.html]
- Activist investor Elliott Management built a stake in BP and pressure mounted for improved returns

**At the 2025 AGM (17 April 2025)**, Auchincloss elaborated: *"In 2020, we'd made some bold strategic changes. Accelerating into the energy transition... We went too far and too fast."* [AGM Speech, Climate Integrity archive: https://climateintegrity.org/uploads/deception/2025-BP-Annual-General-Meeting.pdf]

### 3.3 How BP Framed the Change Publicly

BP framed the purpose change as an evolution **aligned with** rather than **abandoning** its underlying commitment to energy delivery. The press release stated: *"Over the past 12 months, we have worked closely with Murray and his team as they have developed the new direction, ensuring it reflects the significant changes we have seen in energy markets and our purpose of delivering energy to the world, today and tomorrow."* [Helge Lund quote in: https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/growing-shareholder-value-a-reset-bp]

Auchincloss in the reset press release: *"Today we have fundamentally reset BP's strategy. We are reducing and reallocating capital expenditure to our highest-returning businesses to drive growth, and relentlessly pursuing performance improvements and cost efficiency. This is all in service of growing cash flow and returns. This is a reset BP, with an unwavering focus on growing long-term shareholder value."* [CNBC, 26 Feb 2025: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/26/bp-to-ramp-up-fossil-fuel-spending-to-10-billion-in-strategy-reset.html]

Critically, BP maintained its net-zero by 2050 ambition even with the reset: *"Our ambition is to become a net zero company by 2050 or sooner, and to help the world get to net zero."* This line remained unchanged in the "Net Zero Aims at a Glance" document [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/7M1AN8ub2AIK3XyqAOoY0T/8dd2e302efddb46921b641d5e85c636c/bp-net-zero-aims-at-a-glance.pdf] — though BP's specific near-term emissions targets were weakened.

### 3.4 Key Metrics of the Strategic Reset

To contextualise the purpose change within its strategic context:
- Oil and gas investment increased to ~**$10 billion/year** (up ~20%)
- Transition/low-carbon investment reduced to **$1.5–$2 billion/year** (down >$5 billion/year from prior guidance, a >70% cut)
- Oil and gas production target: **2.3–2.5 million barrels of oil equivalent per day by 2030** (vs. former 40% reduction plan)
- Divestment target: **$20 billion by 2027**
- [All figures from: https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/growing-shareholder-value-a-reset-bp]

## QUESTION 4: HISTORICAL PURPOSE/MISSION STATEMENTS

### 4.1 John Browne Era — "Beyond Petroleum" (2000–2007)

**CEO:** Sir/Lord John Browne (CEO 1995–2007)

**"Beyond Petroleum"** was launched on **24 July 2000** as BP's new marketing slogan and rebranding campaign. [BBC, 24 Jul 2000: http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/business/849475.stm] This was NOT a formal "corporate purpose statement" in the modern governance sense — it was a **brand campaign and positioning slogan**, designed by Ogilvy & Mather at a reported cost of $200 million. [PR Watch: https://www.prwatch.org/node/9038]

The rebranding simultaneously changed the company name from "BP Amoco" back to "BP," with the new Helios sunburst logo in green and yellow. The implicit meaning: BP was a company moving beyond petroleum toward solar, renewables, and a broader energy mix.

Key quote from Browne at the launch: BP was seeking to become *"an energy company, not just an oil company"* that *"incorporated solar energy in its portfolio and was willing to move away from oil."* [University of Wollongong / Figshare: https://ro.uow.edu.au/articles/chapter/bp_Beyond_Petroleum_/27690612]

**Criticism:** After the Deepwater Horizon disaster in 2010, critics widely labelled "Beyond Petroleum" as **greenwashing**, arguing the $200 million was spent on image rather than substance. BP quietly dropped the slogan after 2010. [Mother Jones, May 2010: https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2010/05/bp-coated-sludge-after-years-greenwashing/; Forbes 2020: https://www.forbes.com/sites/scottcarpenter/2020/08/04/bps-new-renewables-push-redolent-of-abandoned-beyond-petroleum-rebrand/]

**Note:** Before "Beyond Petroleum," BP operated as "British Petroleum" with no branded purpose statement of the modern governance variety. The Browne era beginning in 1997 marked the first time BP consciously attempted to articulate a broader identity beyond oil.

### 4.2 Tony Hayward Era (2007–2010)

**CEO:** Tony Hayward (CEO 1 May 2007 – 1 October 2010)

Hayward succeeded Browne after Browne was forced out in a scandal. Hayward's tenure was defined by the **Deepwater Horizon disaster** (20 April 2010), the worst offshore oil spill in US history. BP had no notable formal "purpose statement" during this period. Hayward's stated focus was operational safety and simplification: *"safe and reliable operations"* and restoring BP's reputation. He resigned on 27 July 2010 and was replaced by Bob Dudley on 1 October 2010.

### 4.3 Bob Dudley Era — "Focused Oil & Gas" → "Advance Energy" (2010–2020)

**CEO:** Bob Dudley (CEO 1 October 2010 – 31 March 2020)

Dudley's era had **two distinct phases of purpose language:**

**Phase 1 (2010–2017): "Focused oil and gas company"**
Dudley's language during his first years was explicitly reconstructive and operational, not aspiration-driven. In the Q3 2012 earnings call, he stated: *"My vision for BP is a focused oil and gas company that creates value by growing long-term sustainable free cash flow through safe and reliable operations, a disciplined and prudent financial framework, and a portfolio biased to high-margin opportunities."* [Multiple 2012 earnings call transcripts: https://www.roic.ai/quote/BP/transcripts/2012-year/3-quarter] In the 2012 Annual Report summary: *"We aim to create value for shareholders by helping to meet growing demand for energy in a responsible way."* [BP Summary Review 2012: https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/investors/bp-summary-review-2012.pdf]

**Phase 2 (2017–2019): "Dual mission" language and formal purpose**

By 2017-2018, Dudley began using more purposeful language about the energy transition. In an October 2017 speech titled "Advancing the energy transition," he stated: *"Today we are focused on our dual mission of delivering the energy that the world needs while advancing the low carbon world that we all want."* [BP speech archive: https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/news-and-insights/speeches/advancing-the-energy-transition-speech-bob-dudley.pdf]

The **2018 Annual Report** (for financial year 2018) marks a significant milestone: Dudley formally articulated a new corporate purpose, stating explicitly: *"That is why one of the first things I have done with the board is review our purpose in line with our strategy and values. **Our purpose is to advance energy to improve people's lives.**"* [BP Annual Report 2018, confirmed by Investegate reference: https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/1FhAMNbetMqbWhRPL5LMh3/108d032f63dad4ac0cdacac990ca9833/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2018.pdf] The 2018 Annual Report's title was "Growing the business and advancing the energy transition."

This 2018 purpose was thus a **bridge** between the purely commercial Dudley "focused oil and gas" framing and the Looney "reimagining energy" transformation framing. It acknowledged the energy transition but in more measured language.

### 4.4 CEO Timeline Summary

| CEO | Tenure | Nature of Purpose Language |
|-----|--------|---------------------------|
| John Browne | 1995–2007 | "Beyond Petroleum" (brand slogan, not governance purpose) |
| Tony Hayward | 2007–2010 | Operational focus, no formal purpose; safety/reliability |
| Bob Dudley | 2010–2020 | 2010–2017: "focused oil and gas company"; 2018: **"advance energy to improve people's lives"** |
| Bernard Looney | Feb 2020–Sep 2023 | **"Reimagining energy for people and our planet"** |
| Murray Auchincloss (interim) | Sep 2023–Jan 2024 | Same purpose nominally maintained; strategy continuity pledged |
| Murray Auchincloss (permanent) | Jan 2024–Dec 2025 | Purpose changed Feb 26 2025 to **"Delivering energy to the world, today and tomorrow"** |
| Meg O'Neill | From 1 April 2026 | Current purpose maintained |

## QUESTION 5: BP ANNUAL REPORTS 2024 AND 2025

### 5.1 BP Annual Report 2024 (for FY2024, published 11 February 2025)

**Purpose statement in the report:** *"Our purpose is to deliver energy to the world, today and tomorrow."* — This new purpose appears prominently in the report. [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/4YdMeDFNDUgV33gMyfrUK7/e6a7feaf8b863b24e985f8b4550699e9/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2024.pdf]

**Strategic framing in the AR2024:** The report is framed entirely around the reset strategy. The opening reads: *"Growing shareholder value. We are fundamentally resetting bp's strategy. We are reallocating capital to drive growth from our highest returning businesses. And we are focused on driving improved performance. This is all in service of growing long-term shareholder value."*

**Chair Helge Lund's letter (AR2024):** *"Under Murray Auchincloss's leadership, bp has made significant moves, continuing to play its part in supplying the energy the world needs today and helping build out the energy system of tomorrow."* [https://www.bp.com/content/dam/bp/business-sites/en/global/corporate/pdfs/investors/bp-ar2024-chairman-letter.pdf]

**CEO Auchincloss's letter (AR2024):** The report subtitle is *"2024: Laying the foundation for growth"* and the text is fully aligned with the oil and gas reset strategy. [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/30eF1X6SS2q7CtrDTpnLUU/14b0a23aabec51300b4ae10ab1386e3e/bp-fourth-quarter-2024-results.pdf]

**Contradiction/Nuance Flag:** The Annual Report 2024 was published on **11 February 2025** — **15 days before** the Capital Markets Update on February 26 where the strategic reset was formally announced. However, the report already uses the new purpose wording. This strongly suggests that: (a) the AR2024 was finalized knowing the purpose change was coming; and/or (b) the new purpose had been decided at board level before the public investor day. The BP "People and Planet Aims" document dated 26 February 2025 explicitly says it *"reflects changes since 14 March 2024"* [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/1mONt6arxEWFez3nYtupga/c68e1763ba224c58817b226d86c8e086/bp-people-and-planet-aims-at-a-glance.pdf] — suggesting some aim-level updates were already in motion as early as March 2024.

### 5.2 BP Annual Report 2025 (for FY2025, published 6 March 2026)

**Purpose statement:** *"Delivering energy to the world, today and tomorrow."* [https://www.bp.com/en-US/investors/results-reporting-and-presentations/annual-report; full PDF: https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/33M2iHp8A6d07McKzKqLNP/12b5d4eccb4e02093d1ad9efc0d6a746/bp-annual-report-and-form-20f-2025.pdf]

**Strategic framing:** The strategic report in AR2025 states: *"Our strategy helps bp compete and grow value as energy demand evolves and continues to grow, all in service of growing shareholder value and returns."* The strategy is summarized as: *"growing the upstream, focusing the downstream and investing with discipline in transition."* [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/uxr3EtlpC1CL4BHy6swR7/93e017a41d0ec839e813f68e6e28f711/bp-ar2025-strategic-report.pdf]

**Leadership note:** By the time of the AR2025's publication (6 March 2026), Murray Auchincloss had already departed (stepped down 18 December 2025), with Meg O'Neill (formerly of Woodside Energy) appointed as the new CEO effective 1 April 2026. Carol Howle served as interim CEO in the gap period. [https://www.bp.com/press-and-publications/press-releases/bp-plc-announces-leadership-transition]

**Sustainability framing:** The AR2025 Sustainability Report [https://www.bp.com/api/files/6cqieuqhq4no/master/6ISrW0tRFKSHbaBT7yeVNf/18b0bc82f1dcf6b0b4d1df187477f8c8/bp-sustainability-report-2025.pdf] states: *"Our purpose is to deliver energy to the world, today and tomorrow. Our sustainability frame underpins delivery of our strategy and our sustainability aims are focused on the areas that we believe are most relevant to the long-term success of our business."*

## CONTRADICTIONS, UNCERTAINTIES, AND FLAGS FOR THE RESEARCH

1. **The AR2019 anomaly:** The 2019 Annual Report carries the "reimagining energy" purpose statement before Looney's official February 2020 announcement. This is explained by the board-CEO collaborative development process that began in October 2019 when Looney's appointment was announced, but it does mean the purpose pre-dates his official public unveiling.

2. **The AR2024 / CMU date gap:** The 2024 Annual Report (11 Feb 2025) carries the *new* purpose statement 15 days before the Capital Markets Update (26 Feb 2025) where the reset was officially announced. This is unusual and suggests the AR2024 was compiled with pre-knowledge of the upcoming announcement — or that the purpose changed internally before the investor day.

3. **Purpose vs. strategy change:** BP itself and media commentary have framed the February 2025 event primarily as a **strategy reset** rather than a **purpose change**. The purpose wording change has received considerably less analysis in media coverage than the shift back to fossil fuels. BP has framed the new purpose as consistent with its business continuity, not as a retreat from the old purpose.

4. **Net-zero ambition retention:** BP retained its 2050 net-zero ambition alongside the new purpose — creating a tension between a purpose focused on "delivering energy" (which could be read as supply-focused) and a net-zero ambition that requires a fundamental transformation of that supply mix. Critics (e.g., Zero Carbon Analytics [https://zerocarbon-analytics.org/energy/oil-and-gas/bp-is-facing-an-existential-crisis-the-energy-transition-is-not/]) argued this was contradictory.

5. **"Advance energy to improve people's lives" (2018):** This Dudley-era purpose appears in the 2018 Annual Report but is not widely cited in analyses of BP's purpose evolution. It appears to have been a transitional statement that did not gain the same public profile as the Looney or Browne-era language.
